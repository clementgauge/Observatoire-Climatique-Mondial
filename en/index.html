1. Real-Time Atmospheric Observations & Key Indicators (1880–2026)

Since the pre-industrial era (1850–1900), atmospheric carbon dioxide (CO₂) measured at the Mauna Loa Observatory (NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory) has risen from 280 ppm to 426.39 ppm (+52%). Atmospheric methane (CH₄) reaches 1,941.2 ppb. Global mean surface temperature anomaly stands at +1.48 °C to +1.52 °C according to NASA GISS (GISTEMP v4) and Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5).

2. Global Causes of Climate Change by Sector (59.1 GtCO₂eq / yr — IPCC AR6)

  • Electricity & Heat Production (31.4% — 18.56 GtCO₂e/yr): Combustion of thermal coal, fossil gas, and oil for power grids and district heating.
  • Heavy Industry & Chemical Processes (23.8% — 14.07 GtCO₂e/yr): Blast-furnace steelmaking, cement clinker calcination (CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂), petrochemicals, and fugitive methane leaks.
  • Agriculture, Livestock & Deforestation AFOLU (21.6% — 12.76 GtCO₂e/yr): Enteric fermentation from cattle (CH₄), tropical deforestation (Amazon, Congo Basin, Borneo), peatland drainage, and synthetic fertilizers (N₂O).
  • Road, Aviation & Maritime Transport (16.2% — 9.57 GtCO₂e/yr): Passenger cars and SUVs, heavy diesel freight trucks, commercial aviation, and bunker fuel shipping.
  • Urban Waste & Wastewater (7.0% — 4.14 GtCO₂e/yr): Biogenic methane from open landfills and anaerobic wastewater treatment.

3. Global Deforestation (-10 Mha/yr), WWF Wildlife Protection & Climate Policies

Tropical Deforestation (FAO / WWF): 10 million hectares of forest are lost every year across the Amazon & Cerrado (-1.95 Mha/yr), Congo Basin (-0.82 Mha/yr), Borneo & Sumatra (-0.68 Mha/yr), and Gran Chaco (-0.65 Mha/yr).

WWF Wildlife Conservation & Global Policies: Protecting keystone species (forest elephants, jaguars, cetaceans, lynx, polar bears) alongside national and international laws including France’s Low-Carbon Strategy (SNBC), the European Green Deal (Fit for 55, EUDR, CBAM), and the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (30×30).
